# AC-01: Summaries read their Covered Posts

**What to build:** every prompt a Summary gets reads that Summary's Covered Posts and no
others, whoever builds it: Generate, Retry, the scheduler's regeneration, or a copied prompt.
A Summary regenerated by the scheduler gets the same prompt as the same Summary regenerated
from the browser. A Covered Post that can no longer be read (reclaimed, or its Channel
unfollowed) is left out, counted, and shown as an "N missing" chip in the Summary's header
and Scope line. Spec: `.scratch/artifact-coverage/spec.md`; decisions: ADR-038 and
`docs/artifact-coverage-plan.md`.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)
