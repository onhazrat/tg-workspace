How this was measured

Backend complexity is radon's cyclomatic complexity. Coverage is coverage.py line coverage from the full pytest suite (__BACKEND_TESTS__, __BACKEND_LINE_COV__ of lines).

Frontend complexity follows ESLint's complexity rule, counted on the TypeScript AST. Coverage is bun's line coverage from bun test src (__FRONTEND_TESTS__). __FRONTEND_E2E__

Nested functions and closures are scored on their own on both sides, and their lines don't count toward the enclosing function. The generated client, the vendored shadcn components in frontend/src/components/ui, tests and .conform.ts files are excluded.
